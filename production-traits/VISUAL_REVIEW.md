# Visual review — curated 420

The 420-piece selection uses the 18 unmarked fits on the supplied V3 sheet. The exact headwear, skin, expression, and head registration are retained. Other head fits are not approved by association. Thirty-two marked samples are excluded; eleven designs with no surviving fit are absent from production.

All 420 actual exports were visually inspected on the fourteen 30-piece proof sheets at 320-pixel tile size. The eighteen original approved contexts and the 54-piece styling preview were also inspected. Native-size spot checks included the fur/temple and clothing interaction of the ushanka and the tricorn with the spacesuit, plus the original caption readability over light clothing. This is not a claim that every complete 1254-pixel export was individually viewed at native size.

The selection balances all thirteen available designs at 32 or 33 pieces each. Outfits and backgrounds vary around fixed approved heads. Cool skin and cyan trim are echoed in cyan/sky-blue captions; olive and lime palettes use green accents; warm straw, gold and amber contexts use vermilion; coral/red contexts use red; violet/gold contexts use magenta or complementary warm accents. Neutral outfits provide space for the headwear and face to stand out. No obvious color clashes were found in the proof-sheet review. Aesthetic preference remains subjective.

Light garments exposed a readability problem in the transparent caption backing during preview review. Those cases now use the existing solid-backed red/vermilion variants. No text lettering was regenerated. The six chosen original variants preserve their original colors and texture. The unused dark-blue variant remains archived and cannot enter generation.

The 420 compositions are unique before captions: no image is made unique merely by adding or changing text. There are 210 captioned and 210 uncaptioned pieces. Caption glyphs are below y950 and do not cover the face. All 210 pass the recorded native-size local contrast diagnostic; this supplements visual inspection and is not a WCAG certification.

No unresolved technical or fit defect was identified in this restricted selection. Approval of the original 18 head fits is user supplied; the new surrounding styling is the curated selection shown for review. Removed designs shown on the all-24 reference catalog remain excluded from generation.
